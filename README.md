## How it feels

Instead of scrolling a page, you play it. Move the guide with a touch joystick on your phone, WASD or arrow keys on a laptop, or a gamepad. Each object in the world is a section of the portfolio.

## What is inside

- **Projects:** walk up to see what has been built
- **Certificates:** a gallery of credentials
- **LeetCode:** problem-solving progress
- **Contact:** ways to reach Subash

## Controls

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| Phone | On-screen joystick |
| Keyboard | WASD or arrow keys |
| Gamepad | Supported |
